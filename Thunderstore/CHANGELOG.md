# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Ghost and dead-body materials now show on custom skins (the game's own material swap missed them). Ghost materials only while dead (`Materials.GhostOnlyWhenDead`), so living hands stay solid in BuzzWire.
- Change costume mid-match with hotkeys (F6/F7/F10/F11), synced to other players with the mod through the chat.
- Favourites: star button on every wardrobe skin card, favourites pinned to the top, "Favorites" pool for the Random card.
- Card holders can follow a Follow Rig model's own hand (placed on the model's A-pose in the SDK).
- Works with the current HANDS OVER update (new base skins, BuzzWire mode).

## 1.0.0

- First release: skin bundles, outfits, full-body and Follow Rig skins, stumps, random card, held-item offsets, jiggle.
